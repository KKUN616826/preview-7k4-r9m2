Reasoning and action,
in continuous
coordination.

Over a long horizon, a robot must remember task state, act under physical constraints, and use execution feedback to decide what comes next.

Final success alone offers limited insight into where this process breaks down.

One benchmark. Three complementary views.

Mind2Act World is a simulation benchmark for studying task reasoning, physical execution, and their coordination.
