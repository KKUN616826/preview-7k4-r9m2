Instructions and demonstrations

  1. Select a task from the catalog to open its demonstration page.

  2. Switch between Easy, Medium, and Hard to view the corresponding video and instruction.

  3. The instruction panel describes the task operation.

Only the E2 Hard demonstration is pending; all other added task levels have videos available.
