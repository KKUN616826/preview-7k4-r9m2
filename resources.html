Overview

Robotic manipulation requires more than choosing the right goal or executing an isolated skill. Over a long horizon, a robot must remember task state, act under physical constraints, and use execution feedback to decide what comes next. Final success alone offers limited insight into where this process breaks down.

Mind2Act World is a simulation benchmark for studying task reasoning, physical execution, and their coordination. Shared observation and action interfaces, explicit timing conditions, and disclosure of execution assistance support comparisons across VLA and world-action policies, coding agents, and hybrid systems.

Task suites

The benchmark comprises 30 tasks in three suites. Each task has Easy, Medium and Hard configurations that vary memory load, planning dependencies, precision requirements and timing pressure.
