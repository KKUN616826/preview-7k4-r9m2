Dual-Arm Cooking

Remember a recipe and coordinate preparation, cooking, temperature control, and cleanup.

EasyMediumHard

Instruction

Pour each required jar into the pot in the remembered recipe order, returning jars upright and caps to the tray. Turn the black knob to HEAT and press the green button; within 4 s after 80 °C, switch to KEEP WARM, then press it again.

Evaluation dimensions

Recipe sequence memorydual-arm coordinationtemperature feedback and phase progression
