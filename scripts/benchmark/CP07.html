Bridge Construction

Construct a bridge across a gap using the available building pieces.

EasyMediumHard

Instruction

Build a stable bridge across the gap with continuous wheel paths on both sides. Release the bridge pieces, then press and release the button and let the car drive across to the opposite bank.

Task focus

Construction planningPart placementStructural continuity

Instruction

Build a stable bridge across the gap with continuous wheel paths on both sides. Choose suitable pieces by shape and size from the stock, which includes extra blocks, and leave unused blocks aside. Release the bridge pieces, then press and release the button and let the car drive across to the opposite bank.
