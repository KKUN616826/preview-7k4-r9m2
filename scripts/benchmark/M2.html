Memory Card Matching

Remember revealed card identities and locations to find matching pairs.

EasyMediumHard

Instruction

Press and release a card's yellow button to turn it face up, revealing at most two unmatched cards at a time. Matching pictures stay face up; mismatches turn face down automatically. Wait until the flip or pair result finishes before selecting another card. Remember the pictures and their positions and match every pair.
