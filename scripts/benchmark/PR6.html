Domino Chain Bell Ringing

Arrange dominoes into a chain that carries a falling motion to the bell.

EasyMediumHard

Instruction

Stand the dominos anywhere inside the continuous curve band, in order along the curve. Push the first one so the chain of contacts rings the bell. Repair fallen or stalled segments using as few pushes as possible.
