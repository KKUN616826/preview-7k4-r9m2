Keyboard Sequence Reproduction

Remember a demonstrated key sequence, reproduce it with mallets, and return the tools.

EasyMediumHard

Instruction

Pick up the central mallet, press the triangle-marked button and watch the demonstration while holding it, then reproduce the eight key presses in order. Return and release the mallet, release all keys, and withdraw your arms.
