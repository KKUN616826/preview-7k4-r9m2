Rotating Cake Decoration

Remember a decoration layout and reproduce it on a rotating cake while completing the required stages.

EasyMediumHard

Instruction

Observe the stationary decorated cake for three seconds. Remember each macaron and its location, then reproduce the arrangement while the cake rotates. Complete the required lid, start, stop and cover operations.
