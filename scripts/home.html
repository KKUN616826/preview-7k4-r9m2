Why Mind2Act

Final success alone hides where a task breaks down

Robotic manipulation requires more than choosing the right goal or executing an isolated skill. Over a long horizon, a robot must remember task state, act under physical constraints, and use execution feedback to decide what comes next.

Mind2Act World separates these demands into three complementary suites. Each of the __TASK_COUNT__ tasks has Easy, Medium and Hard configurations that vary memory load, planning dependencies, precision and timing pressure. Evaluation combines task success with stage progress and event-level evidence, so errors in goal selection, physical execution and the use of feedback can be told apart.
