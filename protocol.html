Metrics

Every run reports two separate measurements. They are not interchangeable.

Score 0–100
Task performance on a 0–100 scale, combining task success with stage progress.
Success %
The final task success rate: successful trials divided by total trials.
Zero and unreported 0 vs —
A measured zero is a result. A dash means no result has been published yet; models without results are listed as “in evaluation”.

Coverage

The catalogue has 30 tasks. Current results cover 15 of them, five per suite, at all difficulty levels combined. Results for the remaining tasks will be added as they are evaluated.

Comparing models

Coding agents, VLA policies and world-action models differ in how they are trained and how they act. Read any comparison alongside these conditions:

  • Training conditions
  • Exact checkpoint
  • Observation permissions
  • Action interface
  • Execution assistance
  • Evaluation budget

Submitting

Open a submission on GitHub with your model, its checkpoint or harness, and the conditions above. Once it has been evaluated on the same task configurations, it is added to the leaderboard.
